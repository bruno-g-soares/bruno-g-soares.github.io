Privacy Notice

This is a private, non-commercial Actual Budget installation used only by its joint account holders.

With the account holders’ explicit consent, the application retrieves account identifiers, balances, and transaction history from their selected banks through Enable Banking for household budgeting. It does not request payment-initiation access.

Imported financial data is stored on the account holders’ private, self-hosted Actual Budget server. Data is not sold or used for advertising. Enable Banking and the selected banks process information as necessary to provide PSD2 account-information access under their own privacy terms.

The account holders may revoke bank consent, unlink an account, or delete imported data at any time. Privacy enquiries may be sent to the data-protection email registered for this application with Enable Banking.

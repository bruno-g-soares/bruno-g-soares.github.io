Terms of Use

This application is a private, non-commercial household budgeting tool for its joint account holders. It is not offered as a service to the public.

Bank connectivity is used only to retrieve account information, balances, and transactions after explicit authorization. The application does not provide payment-initiation services.

Bank-sync availability and data accuracy depend on the selected banks and Enable Banking. The account holders remain responsible for verifying imported information against official bank records.

Use of bank connectivity is also subject to the applicable bank terms, Enable Banking terms, and PSD2 consent presented during authorization. Access may be revoked and linked accounts removed at any time.
